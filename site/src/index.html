Before you install

Can I transfer Spotify playlists to Apple Music or YouTube Music?

Yes. Connect your accounts, open Transfers, and choose the source and destination. SongMirror shows unmatched tracks so you can review them.

Can I keep the same playlist updated across multiple services?

Yes. Create a scheduled sync and choose one-way, multi-source merge, authoritative-group, or bidirectional sync. Preview planned additions and removals before enabling writes.

Where does SongMirror store my account settings?

Settings, tokens, match history, and playlist backups stay on the machine running SongMirror. The public website only serves the setup guides and trailer.

Can I use my Jellyfin or Navidrome library?

Yes. SongMirror syncs playlists with supported servers and matches incoming tracks against audio they already have. For a separate offline audio copy, use the local Jellyfin-ready download mirror.
